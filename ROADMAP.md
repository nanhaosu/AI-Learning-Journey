August 19-27, 2026.

## Objective

Connect the core practical knowledge covered by Hung-yi Lee's machine learning theory lectures and the practical competency map associated with Dive into Deep Learning. Instruction is provided directly and interactively by Codex; watching Li Mu's videos is not required.

## Daily Rhythm

- Concept explanation: 45 minutes
- Minimal experiments: 60 minutes
- Main implementation: 2.5 hours
- Independent rewrite or completion: 1.5 hours
- Comparison experiments: 1 hour
- README and GitHub record: 45 minutes
- Understanding check: 30 minutes

## Rules

- Quality gates remain mandatory even during the sprint.
- If a prerequisite gap blocks the next topic, repair it immediately and compress nonessential details rather than hiding the gap.
- Research-direction material is outside this sprint unless it is explicitly requested.
- Daily progress is judged by executable code, independent explanation, modification ability, and experiment records rather than video or chapter completion.
