# Learning Profile

## Background

- Undergraduate student who has recently joined a trustworthy AI research group.
- Theoretical study follows Hung-yi Lee's machine learning lectures.
- Current theoretical progress has reached GANs, but practical ability is still at the PyTorch fundamentals stage.
- Li Mu's practical course is treated only as a knowledge-coverage reference. Daily practical teaching is reorganized and taught interactively by Codex.

## Current Priority

Build a connected practical foundation from classification training through CNNs, sequence models, attention, Transformers, autoencoders, and GANs by August 27, 2026.

The immediate curriculum does not revolve around the research topic. Trustworthy AI, data poisoning, and backdoor defense will be addressed after the core practical foundation is established.

## Study Constraints

- At least 6 hours of practical study per day; the sprint assumes roughly 7-9 hours when possible.
- Do not require watching Li Mu's videos.
- Teach concepts from the learner's current level instead of copying a textbook chapter sequence.
- Prefer explanation, small experiments, learner-written code, independent rewriting, comparison experiments, and GitHub documentation.
- Do not equate a numbered Day with mastery of an entirely new architecture.

## Teaching Protocol

1. Check prerequisite understanding.
2. Explain the concept with a minimal example.
3. Ask the learner to write the key code.
4. Give progressive hints instead of an immediate complete solution.
5. Run at least one comparison experiment.
6. Verify understanding with explanation and independent modification.
7. Record code, results, errors, and remaining questions in the repository.
